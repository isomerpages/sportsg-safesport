<h4>As the Safe Sport Commission marks its 5th anniversary, we are gathering feedback on all the elements of the framework, training initiatives, case management and policy implementation.</h4>
<p><strong>We would love to hear from you.</strong> If you have engaged with
the programme, your perspective is invaluable to us.</p>
<p>We invite you to sign up for voluntary <strong>focus group discussions</strong> to
share your thoughts in greater depth. Each focus group participant will
receive a token of appreciation for their time. You may participate in
more than one discussion topic.</p><a class="isomer-image-wrapper" href="go.gov.sg/ss-5yr-fgd"><img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Focus group flyer featuring colorful graphics and details about participation and activities." src="/images/SS_5YR_FGD_Topics_Infographic.png"></a>
<p>If you are keen to participate, please register your interest <a href="" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">here</a> by 26 June 2026.</p>
